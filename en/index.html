Referral (20%)

A hot lead is someone with a call booked, not a name on a list.

What turns a contact into a referral is a booked call: the client has given a day, times and their contact details. The five points below earn the 20%.

  1. They are the owner or the decision-maker.Not an employee, not the owner's nephew.
  2. They have a problem they said out loud.No website, an old website, losing clients by answering slowly, wanting online booking.
  3. They've seen the price list and didn't find it unrealistic.So nobody is surprised by the price in the meeting.
  4. They agreed to a 20-minute call and gave a day and times.Within the next 7 days, by video or phone. No day and time, no referral yet.
  5. You sent the form with their phone and email.We confirm the time, put it on the calendar and send the invite. The form is at the end of this page.

Not a hot lead

  • “My cousin might want a website someday.”
  • A contact list or a group chat.
  • Someone who doesn't know the price yet.
  • “Call me any time”, with no time or contact details.
  • Someone already talking to us.

These can become hot leads. Talk to them, show the price and, if they agree to the call, send the form.

How to register your client

Fill in the form at the end of this page with the client's day, times and contact details, and email it to us. Within 1 business day we confirm the time with them and send the invite.

Want to join the call? You can if you like. It's not required: just tick it on the form.

From then on, they're yours for 90 days. If they close in that time, the commission is yours, even if the decision takes a while. If two partners refer the same person, whoever registered first wins.

Full sale (50%)

The client arrives decided, with everything ready for us to start.

To earn the 50%, the five points below have to be done. If one is missing, we hand it back to you to finish before the project starts.

  1. The client chose the package and accepted the price.The list price, or the quote for the full combo. Discounts only with our OK.

Questions clients will ask

Short answers to the usual questions.

Don't know an answer? Say “let me check with the team” and ask us. Better than making something up.

“Why not build it myself on Wix?”

You can. The difference is that we write the copy, set the site up to bring in clients through Google, calls and texts, and look after it afterwards. You don't lose a weekend fiddling with a website.

“How long does it take?”

The Showcase Site goes live within 5 business days of the deposit and the completed questionnaire. The AI assistant takes up to 10, the website with CRM up to 20, and the full combo's timeline is in the quote.

“How do I pay?”

50% up front to start and 50% when you approve the project, straight to Anthracite.

“Do I have to pay a monthly fee?”

Not for the Showcase Site: it stays live at no cost, and the $29 a month upkeep plan is optional. The AI assistant, the website with CRM and the combo include a monthly fee, with no lock-in.

“Is the website mine?”

Yes. It lives in an account in your name, and we're added as administrators to look after it. Want your own .com address? We'll explain how to register one: about $15 a year.

“What if I don't like it?”

You have 30 days after the project goes live. We fix any problem or refund your money.

“What if I want to change something later?”

With the upkeep plan, small changes are included: menu, prices, photos, information. Without it, we quote each request. A new page or a new site is always quoted separately.

“Will the AI say something silly to my clients?”

It only answers with information you approved, introduces itself as a digital assistant and hands you anything it doesn't know or that needs a person.

“Does it answer texts and calls?”

Yes. The assistant answers text messages, phone calls and the website chat, around the clock, and always says it's a digital assistant.

“Do you have clients?”

Yes, businesses in the United States, which are in the portfolio with their sites live. The demos show what can be done in your line of business.

“Can I talk to someone before deciding?”

Of course. We'll set up a 20-minute call with Juliana or Sean.

“Who is Anthracite?”

Anthracite AI builds smart websites and AI agents for small and mid-sized businesses, and does AI consulting. Based in Charlotte, NC.

Partnership terms

The agreement, in writing.

To protect you and Anthracite. By sending your first referral form, you agree to these terms.

Read the partnership terms
  1. What counts as a referral. The form must include the client's phone and email and a day with times for the call. With that, Anthracite books the call and sends the invite. Without those details, the contact is not a referral yet. The partner joining the call is optional.
  2. Commission only on a completed sale. You are paid once the client has paid for the whole project and it is live. An accepted proposal, a paid deposit or “they're about to sign” does not earn a commission yet.
  3. Paid after the guarantee. The commission is paid in one go, by bank transfer, within 7 days after the project has been live for 30 days. If the client asks for a refund during the guarantee, there is no commission.
  4. How long this offer runs. It covers clients registered by January 15, 2027 and closed within 90 days of registration. Registrations after that date follow whatever terms are in place then.
  5. What the commission is based on. The percentage applies to the project price the client actually paid. Monthly fees, domain and other charges are not included.
  6. The client pays Anthracite only. If a partner takes any money from the client, the commission on that sale is cancelled and the partnership may be ended.
  7. Registration and exclusivity. The client is yours for 90 days from the day we confirm the registration. The first partner to register wins. Contacts already talking to Anthracite earn no commission.
  8. No employment relationship. The partnership creates no employment, partnership in the company or agency. A partner does not sign, promise or charge on Anthracite's behalf.
  9. Promises. Price, timeline, discount and scope are as stated in this guide or as Anthracite approves in writing. Anthracite is not bound by promises made outside that.
  10. Anthracite may turn a project down. For lack of capacity, because of the scope or because of the client's line of business. A declined project earns no commission.
  11. Client data. Only pass on the contact details of someone who agreed to it. The data is used for this referral only and is never shared with third parties, in line with applicable privacy laws.
  12. Use of the brand. Only with Anthracite's own materials and pages. Paid ads, profiles or your own materials using the Anthracite name need written approval.
  13. Taxes and payment details. Each party handles its own taxes. To be paid, the partner provides a completed IRS Form W-9 and bank details in their own name.
  14. Ending the partnership. Either side can end the partnership at any time with a message. Commissions on sales already completed are still paid under these terms.
  15. Changes. Anthracite may change these terms for new registrations, with notice. Anything already registered keeps the rules in force when it was registered.
  16. Everything in writing. Registrations, approvals and agreements count when they are in writing, including by text or email.
